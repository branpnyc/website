Selected work / Evidence

Projects where learners made the tools their own.

01

Printed proof

Context

Students turned ideas, interviews, and visual choices into a shared publication.

Learner action

They shaped pages, edited copy, and made design choices for a real audience.

Outcome

The zine made media production tangible: layout, editing, authorship, and audience.

Open zine
02

Public voice

Context

Community conversation became an audio project with structure, pacing, and purpose.

Learner action

They planned segments, handled recording tools, and practiced listening as production work.

Outcome

Learners practiced listening, editing, hosting, and shaping a story people could hear.

Open podcast
03

Fulfillment through film

Context

Documentary work gave students a way to question stereotypes through lived experience.

Learner action

They used interviews, location, and image choices to make a claim with evidence.

Outcome

The final film connected place, memory, voice, and evidence in a public archive.

Open film
04

Art for all

Context

Youth writing moved from private reflection into a public digital space.

Learner action

They drafted posts, revised ideas, and used publishing tools to invite response.

Outcome

Learners practiced voice, revision, and publishing with a tool they could keep using.

Open blog
